About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a minimalist tool built to help you capture fleeting thoughts and important reminders instantly. Whether you are brainstorming a new project or just making a grocery list, we make sure your ideas are safely stored.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Navigate to the Home page and locate the input box.
  2. Type out your brilliant idea, task, or reminder.
  3. Press the "Add Note" button to save it to your list.

Features

  • Instant and distraction-free note creation
  • Clean, accessible user interface
  • Secure local storage directly in your browser
